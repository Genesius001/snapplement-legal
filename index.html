AI read the label incorrectly

Compare the result with the package and correct any unclear field before saving. A clearer front photo and Supplement Facts photo may improve the next attempt.

Where is my stack stored?

Confirmed details and saved label photos are stored in the app on your device. Photos used for AI recognition are sent to Snapplement's backend and Google Cloud Vertex AI.

What is included with Free?

Two confirmed AI label scans are included. Manual entry and products already saved in your Free stack remain available.

Billing or Restore Purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Manage or cancel an active subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings.

Is this medical advice?

No. Snapplement organizes label information. It does not choose supplements, determine a medically appropriate dose, assess interactions, diagnose, or treat.
