Snapplement Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026 · Griffonix Inc.

Summary

Snapplement is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not require an account, we do not sell data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

What we process and why

Bottle photos. When you snap or select a photo of a supplement bottle, it is transmitted securely to our server and processed by Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) to identify the product. Photos are processed in memory for recognition only and are not retained on our servers after processing. You are asked for consent in the app before any photo is processed.

Your supplement list and schedule. Stored locally on your device only. We do not receive or store it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Device identifier. The app generates a random identifier (not Apple's advertising ID) used solely to prevent abuse of our recognition service (rate limiting).

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status. We never see your payment details.

What we do NOT collect

No name, email, contacts, location, health records, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. No analytics SDKs are included in the current version. If this changes, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.

Third-party processors

Google Cloud (Vertex AI — photo recognition, server hosting), Apple (payments), RevenueCat (subscription status). Each receives only the minimum described above.

Your choices and rights

You can deny camera/photo access (the app then has limited functionality), delete the app to remove all local data, and manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) you may have additional rights — contact us to exercise them.

Children

Snapplement is intended for users 18 years or older and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with an updated effective date.
