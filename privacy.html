Snapplement · Griffonix Inc.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Snapplement does not require an account, sell personal data, or use personal data for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising. Confirmed label details and saved bottle photos are stored in the app on your device. When you choose to scan a label, the selected photos leave your device and are processed by Snapplement's backend and Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) to return a recognition result.

1. Scope and controller

This Policy explains how Griffonix Inc. ("Griffonix," "we," "us") processes information when you use the Snapplement mobile application, recognition service, public support and legal pages, and related subscription features.

2. Information kept on your device

Snapplement stores the following information in the app's storage on your device:

  • product, brand, serving-size, ingredient, amount, category, and review details you confirm or enter;
  • front and back label photos saved with confirmed products;
  • your photo-processing consent timestamp;
  • a random installation identifier;
  • the number of included AI confirmations used and local subscription-access state.

Snapplement does not offer an account or cloud sync in this release. Removing individual products removes their app records. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to your device backup, file-system, and operating-system behavior.

3. AI label recognition

When you ask Snapplement to recognize a label, the app sends the selected front photo, optional back photo, and a random installation identifier over an encrypted connection to Snapplement's backend. The backend sends the photos to Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) to extract visible label information.

Snapplement's backend processes submitted photos in memory and does not persist the raw photos after the request completes. The current release configuration does not enable the optional server-side recognition-result cache. If we enable server-side result caching or materially change this retention practice, we will update this Policy before doing so.

Google states that it does not use customer data to train its models without permission. Google may retain limited prompts, outputs, or service data for security and abuse monitoring under its applicable terms and retention controls.

AI recognition may be incomplete or incorrect. Snapplement asks you to review and, where necessary, correct the extracted details before saving them.

4. Service limits and operational data

The random installation identifier is not Apple's advertising identifier. Snapplement's backend uses it to apply recognition limits and prevent abuse. In the current architecture, request counts are held in the memory of a backend instance and are not written to Snapplement's application logs.

To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, we process limited technical data such as request time, number and byte size of photos, detected file type, request outcome, generic error type, latency, model token counts, confidence, generic result category, and ingredient count. Snapplement's application logs are designed not to include submitted photos, product names, brands, ingredient names, raw model responses, or the random installation identifier. Operational logs are retained for no longer than 30 days unless a longer period is required to investigate a security incident or comply with law.

Google Cloud Run request logs also contain standard connection information, including IP address, user agent, request URL, status, and latency, to deliver, monitor, and protect the service. These request logs are stored in the project's default Cloud Logging bucket, which is currently configured for a 30-day retention period.

5. Subscriptions

Apple processes purchases and payment details. Snapplement uses RevenueCat to retrieve subscription products, determine access, process purchase status, restore purchases, and provide subscription analytics and customer history. RevenueCat may process an anonymous App User ID, receipt and transaction information, subscription status, and limited device or operating-system information. Snapplement does not receive your full payment-card number.

6. Support communications

If you contact support, we process the email address, message, and any information you choose to include so we can respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive information that is not needed for support.

7. Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers only for the purposes described above:

  • Google Cloud — backend hosting, operational logging, security, and Vertex AI label recognition;
  • Apple — app distribution, device permissions, and payments;
  • RevenueCat — subscription catalog, entitlement, purchase, and restore functionality and subscription analytics;
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the public support, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service pages.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal data.

8. Retention and deletion

  • App data and saved label photos remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app.
  • Raw photos submitted for recognition are not persisted by Snapplement's backend after the request completes.
  • Snapplement operational logs are kept for no longer than 30 days, except as described above.
  • Apple, Google Cloud, and RevenueCat retain information under their own terms and legal obligations.
  • Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

9. Your choices and rights

You can deny camera or photo-library access, stop using AI recognition, use manual entry, remove saved products, delete the app's local data, manage your subscription in your Apple Account, and contact us about access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or other privacy rights available in your region. You can stop future photo processing at any time by not submitting another photo and by revoking camera or photo-library access in system settings; this does not affect processing already completed.

Because Snapplement has no user account, we may need limited information to locate a support message or third-party subscription record. We cannot remotely delete data stored only on your device.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are handled using contractual or other legally recognized safeguards.

11. Age

Snapplement is intended for people aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16. If we learn that we processed information from a child under 16 contrary to applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network transport and data-minimizing application logs. No system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes

We may update this Policy as the service or law changes. We will post the revised version here with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required.
